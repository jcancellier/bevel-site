Questions

How do I aim?

Touch or drag toward where you want the ball to go, and let go to fire. Bevel has four ways to aim: Point, Classic (pull back and let go), Anchor below, and Aim & Shoot (aim, adjust with the arrow buttons, then tap Shoot). Pick one in Settings → Controls, or see them all in How to play.

How do I cancel a shot?

Before you let go, follow the hint by your finger: with Point, move back onto the ball and let go; with Classic, slide back; with Anchor below, slide up. With Aim & Shoot nothing fires until you tap Shoot, so just aim again.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases (the starter pack also has a Restore link in the shop). Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I remove ads?

Buy Remove ads in Settings. It's a one-time purchase that turns off all ads on every device with your Apple Account, and video offers like One more shot then need no video. For young players, No ad buttons in Settings hides every "watch an ad" button and puts buying behind a grown-up's 2-second hold.

How do I reset my progress?

Settings → Reset progress. It clears every room, star, daily room, your tower, your coins and the looks bought with coins, and starts you at room 1. It can't be undone. Things bought with real money stay, and so do your settings.

Can I play in another language?

Bevel follows your iPhone's language. It speaks English, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese, German, French, Korean, Italian, Traditional Chinese, Turkish and Indonesian. To use a different one for Bevel only, open the iPhone's Settings → Apps → Bevel → Language.

My progress is gone after a new phone

Progress is saved on your phone and moves with an iCloud or computer backup restore. Purchases always come back with Restore purchases.
